TUXI Global Marketplace — Privacy & Data Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Version: 2.4

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TUXI Global Marketplace ("TUXI", "we", "us") collects, protects, processes, and deletes personal information when you access or use our applications across Microsoft Store, Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and Web.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, verified email address, phone number, and physical delivery address.
  • Precise Geolocation: Real-time coordinates during active order transit for driver dispatch and live radar navigation.
  • Payment Information: Processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors (Stripe / Apple Pay / Google Pay). We never store raw credit card numbers.
  • Biometric Data: WebAuthn Passkeys (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello) are processed strictly on-device in your hardware Secure Enclave. No biometric data is ever sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Proof of Delivery & Documents: Photos of package drop-offs, restaurant hygiene certifications, and driver onboarding documentation.

2. Background Location Disclosure (Google Play & Apple Review Mandate)

TUXI collects location data from active delivery couriers to enable real-time order tracking for customers, compute fuel-efficient route navigation, and calculate live ETAs, even when the app is running in the background or when the screen is locked. Zero tracking occurs when the driver is offline.

3. Data Protection & Security

All network communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. Stored records and cloud backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.

4. Data Retention & Right to Erasure (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) & GDPR)

Users can export their personal data or permanently delete their account and associated records directly inside the TUXI app at any time via the in-app Account Deletion Portal, or by contacting privacy@tuxi-marketplace.com.
